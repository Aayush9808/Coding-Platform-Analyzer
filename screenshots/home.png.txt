# Screenshot Placeholders

The images in this directory are placeholders. 

## To Add Real Screenshots:

1. Visit https://coding-platform-analyzer.vercel.app
2. Take screenshots of:
   - Home page (home.png)
   - Dashboard with results (dashboard.png)
   - Charts section (charts.png)
   - AI insights (ai-insights.png)
   - Export buttons (export.png)

3. Replace the placeholder text files with actual PNG images

## Quick Screenshot Guide:

**Windows:** Press `Win + Shift + S` to capture

**Mac:** Press `Cmd + Shift + 4` to capture

**Browser:** Press `F12` > `Ctrl+Shift+P` > Type "Capture screenshot"

See screenshots/README.md for detailed instructions.
